# Taskail ⌘K

**Catch every task at the speed of thought.**

Taskail is a high-performance desktop productivity tool designed for builders, thinkers, and busy professionals. It bridges the gap between a fleeting idea and an organized list using a seamless **Alt+K** hotkey and instant AI voice capture.

## 🚀 The Alt+K Workflow
Most task managers require you to stop what you're doing, open an app, and type. Taskail stays out of your way until you need it.

1.  **Press `Alt + K`** (from any app, anytime).
2.  **Speak your task** (e.g., *"Remind me to call the client at 3 PM tomorrow"*).
3.  **Release.** Taskail's local AI parses your voice, extracts the deadline, and adds it to your list instantly.

## ✨ Key Features
*   **Zero-Friction Capture**: A global hotkey allows for instant recording without switching windows.
*   **Privacy-First AI**: Powered by OpenAI's Whisper (Tiny) running **entirely on your local machine**. Your voice data never leaves your computer.
*   **Smart Parsing**: Automatically detects priorities and due dates from your natural speech.
*   **Ambient UI**: A sleek, glassmorphic interface that feels like a native part of your OS.
*   **Statistical Insights**: Track your productivity rate with built-in completion analytics.

## 📦 How to Install
1.  Go to the [Latest Release](https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/taskail/releases).
2.  Download **`taskail Setup 1.0.0.exe`**.
3.  Run the installer.
4.  Press **`Alt + K`** to start capturing.

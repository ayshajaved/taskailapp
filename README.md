# Taskail ⌘K
# App will be released in a couple of day(Fixing some issues)
**Catch every task at the speed of thought.**
### Imagine this...
You are scrolling on LinkedIn and suddenly remember you have to upload an assignment in 2 hours. You fear you will forget. Or you're watching a series, learn a new word, and want to save it without switching to a note-taking app.

**What if you could press a hotkey (Alt + K), speak the task, and have it remind you—without ever switching your app?**

Taskail solves the "Context Switch Tax" (the 12 seconds it takes to switch apps where 47% of tasks are forgotten).

---

## 🚀 The Alt+K Solution
Taskail is the simplest solution to a universal problem: capturing thoughts before they vanish.

*   **Anywhere on Windows**: Press `Alt + K` and speak.
*   **Smart Reminders**: Desktop + Voice reminders that keep alerting you until acknowledged.
*   **Categorization**: It automatically sorts your voice into **#tasks**, **#priority**, or **#notes**.

## ✨ Key Features
*   **Zero-Friction Capture**: No app switching. Press, speak, added.
*   **Offline AI**: Powered by a local Whisper model. No internet? No problem.
*   **Privacy-First**: Local storage only. No cloud, no tracking.
*   **Modern UI**: Sleek, glassmorphic design that feels native.
